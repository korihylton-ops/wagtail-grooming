Frequently Asked

Do you groom anxious or senior pets?

Yes — we specialise in gentle handling for anxious, senior, and first-time grooming pets, with extra time built into the appointment.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most breeds do best every 6-8 weeks, though long-haired or double-coated breeds may need more frequent visits.

Do you use cage dryers?

Never — every pet is hand-dried with a groomer present the whole time.

Can I book online?

Yes, use the booking form below or call us directly — same-week appointments are usually available.
